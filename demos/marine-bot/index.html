    Thresholds after
    Beaufort and Douglas conventions

    A forecast, not an observed sea state, and not a safety authority. It does not replace official marine warnings, harbour guidance, lifeguard flags or your own judgement at the water’s edge. Algeria’s ONM publishes no machine-readable warning feed, so official warnings are not checked here: they are reported as unchecked, never as clear.

    The thresholds are starting assumptions, not local observation. Wave height, period and sea temperature have no public observation on this coast and remain unverified.
