In production a model rewrites the reason lines into better prose, and cannot change a rating: the ratings are re-stamped from this assessment after its response is parsed. This is the deterministic rendering, also what is sent whenever the model fails any of its four output gates.

What the models disagree about

One model’s number is not the answer. The engine queries several and combines them in whichever direction is conservative for each variable: highest waves, shortest period, strongest gusts. Where they disagree beyond a threshold, the rating is held at CAUTION and the report says so.
