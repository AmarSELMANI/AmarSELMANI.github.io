What the models actually disagree about

One model's number is not the answer. The engine queries several and combines them in whichever direction is conservative for each variable — highest waves, shortest period, strongest gusts. Where they disagree beyond a threshold, the rating is held at CAUTION and the report says so.

The message a subscriber receives

In production a model rewrites the reason lines into better prose — and cannot change a rating, because the ratings are re-stamped from this assessment after its response is parsed. This is the deterministic rendering, which is also what gets sent whenever the model fails any of its four output gates.

Forecast only, not an observed sea state, and not a safety authority. It does not replace official marine warnings, harbour guidance, lifeguard flags, or your own judgement at the water's edge. Algeria's ONM publishes no machine-readable warning feed, so official warnings are not checked here — reported as unchecked rather than clear.

Weather and marine data from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Thresholds are starting assumptions from Beaufort and Douglas conventions, not local observation. Wave height, period and sea temperature have no public observation on this coast and remain unverified.
