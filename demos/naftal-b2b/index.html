Try it yourself

Four steps to break it.

1 In the app Hit the credit ceiling Sign in as demo / demo1234. Open Tires → Light Tires → Continental → ULTRACONTACT and ask for 12: at 162,000 DA, “Buy on credit” stays shut. Open the app
2 In the console Free the credit Sign in as admin / demo1234 and, under Orders, confirm the 156,000 DA cheque Atlas declared. A declared payment frees nothing until an agent confirms it. Open the console
3 In the app Order again Reload the app: 306,000 DA is now available, and the same order goes through with a 30-day deadline counting down under My orders. Back to the app
4 Both Open a new account Log out and apply as a new company with the specimen documents below. Approve it in the console and watch the phone move on the moment you click. Get the documents

A declared payment frees nothing until an agent confirms it.

Both clients are the project’s own builds. Their requests go to an in-browser port of the API: the same routes, error codes and rules, including the transactional credit check, declared-then-confirmed payments and single-use refresh tokens.
