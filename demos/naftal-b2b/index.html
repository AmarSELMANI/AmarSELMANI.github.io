Live demo · Mobile app & agent console

Naftal B2B

Companies apply for an account, a Naftal agent reviews their documents, and approved companies order tires and lubricants — paying now, or on credit under a ceiling that holds even when orders race. This is the real app and the real console; their API is answered by a port of the backend running in your browser, so nothing you type or upload leaves this page.

Open the agent console ↗ Open the app →

  1. Hit the credit ceiling

    In the app, sign in as demo / demo1234. Atlas Transport has 150,000 DA left of its 1,250,000 DA ceiling. Open Tires → Light Tires → Continental → ULTRACONTACT and ask for 12: at 162,000 DA, “Buy on credit” stays shut.

  2. Free the credit from the console

    Open the agent console, sign in as admin / demo1234, and under Orders confirm the 156,000 DA cheque Atlas declared. A declared payment frees nothing until an agent confirms it — that is how cheques work in B2B.

  3. Order again

    Reload the app: 306,000 DA is now available, and the same order goes through with a 30-day deadline counting down under My orders.

  4. Or open an account

    Log out and apply as a new company, attaching the specimen documents below. Approve it in the console and watch the phone: it moves on the moment you click.

How it runs. Both clients are the project’s own builds. Their requests go to an in-browser port of the API: the same routes, error codes and rules — the transactional credit check, declared-then-confirmed payments, single-use refresh tokens. Data lives in this browser’s storage, shared by the app and the console, and “Reset demo data” starts it over.
